Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

WildGeoTag is a camera app by RESQ Foundation for wildlife rescue and field work. This page explains what the app uses on your phone and where that information goes.

The short version

Your photos, videos, location and notes are stored on your phone. They leave it only when you share them or make a report. There is no account, no advertising and no analytics, and the app sends nothing to RESQ: we do not receive your photos, videos, location or notes.

What the app uses

  • Camera: to take photos and videos.
  • Precise location (GPS): to stamp where each photo or video was taken. The position comes from your phone's location service (GPS satellites and, on most phones, Google Play services). It is used only while the app is open on the screen, never in the background.
  • Microphone: for sound in videos. If you do not allow it, videos are saved without sound.
  • Compass and motion sensors: to show the direction on the stamp and the level line.

Where your information goes

  • Photos and videos are saved in your phone's Gallery, in the album "RESQ Geotag".
  • The location, date, time, direction and your note are printed on the photo and saved inside the photo file. When you were walking or driving, your speed is saved in the file too (and printed only if you choose it). Anyone you give the photo to can see them. Videos show the same details, second by second.
  • Like photos from the phone's own camera, the photo file also keeps the phone's make and model and the camera settings (exposure, ISO, lens). The app never adds the phone's serial number or any other ID of the phone or of you.
  • Place names come from a list inside the app (Maharashtra). The app sends no location anywhere to look them up.
  • If you turn on "Also save without stamp" (Pro), each photo and video is also saved in the same album exactly as the camera took it, without the stamp. The photo copy keeps the same details inside the file; the video copy has no location inside it.
  • Your settings, and a logo you add to the stamp, are kept only in the app on your phone. They are not included in the phone's backup.

When something leaves your phone

Only in these cases, and only when you choose to:

  • Sharing: when you tap the share button, the photo and its map link go to the app you pick (for example WhatsApp). For protected animals the shared copy shows only the district, with no exact location.
  • Sharing from the Gallery: a photo or video you share from the phone's Gallery is the original, with the exact location, also for protected animals. The app has no share button for videos.
  • Photo list export: when you tap "Make report", a table and a map file of your photos go to the app you pick. They include the exact location of every photo, also of protected animals.
  • Map links and QR codes: when someone opens the map link or scans the QR code on a photo, Google Maps receives those coordinates. See Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

Deleting your information

Delete photos and videos from your Gallery as usual. Uninstalling the app removes its settings. If your Gallery is backed up (for example to Google Photos), the copies there follow that service's settings. We hold no copy.

Children

The app is made for adults working in the field. It does not knowingly collect any information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be in the app and on this page, with a new date at the top.

How accurate the location is

The location comes from your phone's GPS. On every photo the app shows the phone's own estimate of its accuracy (for example "± 4.2 m"); the real error can be larger. It depends on the phone, the sky and the surroundings. The app never makes up a location: when there is no fresh GPS reading, the photo says so instead of showing coordinates. Still, we cannot promise that a location, altitude, direction, time or place name is correct. Place names are the nearest village or town and are approximate.

Using photos as evidence

The app records what the phone reports at the moment the photo is taken. If a photo is used in an official report or in court, check it as you would any other evidence. The stamp and the details inside a file do not prove that a photo was not edited later.

Be careful when sharing the exact location of rare or endangered animals: anyone who sees the photo can find the place.

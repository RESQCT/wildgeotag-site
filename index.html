What it does

Works without a network

GPS, the stamp and place names for Maharashtra work deep inside the forest, with no signal.

Honest location

Shows the accuracy your phone reports. With no fresh GPS reading the photo says so instead of showing a position, and locations Android marks as fake are not stamped.

Protects rare animals

If your note names a protected species, the share button sends a copy that shows only the district, not the exact place.

Animal names in your language

Type an animal’s name in English or an Indian language (naag, बिबट्या) and pick it for the note.

Photos stay yours

No account, no ads, no analytics. Photos and videos are saved in your phone’s Gallery; nothing is sent to us.

Photos and videos

The same stamp on photos and on videos, second by second, with place, date, time, direction and your note.

The accuracy shown is the phone’s own estimate; the real error can be larger. A stamp does not prove that a photo was not edited. Details: Privacy policy · Terms of use.
